About PGC

Prominent General Contractors LLC is a Houston remodeling firm with a deliberate scope of work — kitchens, bathrooms, showers, cabinetry, countertops, interiors, finish carpentry, tile, and specialty flooring through our PGC Flooring division.

We work with homeowners and property owners who value organized communication over speed-of-sale, and craftsmanship over the cheapest available bid. Each engagement is led directly by ownership, with one point of contact and a written scope before any deposit is taken.

Our reputation in the neighborhoods we serve — Kingwood, Atascocita, Humble, Fall Creek, Summerwood, Eagle Springs and Lake Houston — is built on three habits: showing up on time, leaving the home cleaner than we found it, and being honest about what a project will actually take.

04 — Approach

A clear, phased process — and a payment structure that follows the work.

Projects are organized around defined milestones rather than open-ended billing. You see exactly what is happening, when it's happening, and what is being paid for at each step. Payments are accepted via Zelle, ACH, and credit or debit card through Stripe.

— Phase 01

Consultation & Scope

An on-site visit, measured drawings where useful, material direction, and a written proposal that defines the scope before anything is signed.

— Phase 02

Initial Deposit

A modest deposit reserves your project window and begins material specification. Schedule, lead times and prep work are confirmed in writing.

— Phase 03

Material Delivery & Progress

Materials arrive, the workspace is prepared, and installation begins. Progress payment is tied to a clear milestone in the work — never to a calendar.

— Phase 04

Completion & Final Walk

A walk-through, punch list, clean handover and final payment. Warranty terms are documented and the relationship continues for any future work.
