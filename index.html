About PGC

Prominent General Contractors LLC is a Houston general contracting firm built for clients who need real construction done right — commercial improvements, structural work, and residential remodeling handled under one organized contractor.

We work with commercial property owners, business operators, property managers, and homeowners who value clear scopes, accountable scheduling, and professional execution over the cheapest available bid. Every engagement is contractor-led, with a written scope before any deposit is taken.

Our commercial scope covers tenant improvements, office build-outs, commercial painting and drywall, demolition, door installation, TPO roofing, structural repairs, restroom renovations, ADA improvements, and property maintenance. Our residential work spans kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, cabinetry, tile, carpentry, and full interior remodels — delivered through the same organized process we bring to every commercial project.

05 — Process

A clear, phased process — and a payment structure that follows the work.

Commercial or residential — every project is organized around defined milestones rather than open-ended billing. You see exactly what is happening, when it is happening, and what is being paid for at each step. Payments accepted via Zelle, ACH, and credit or debit card through Stripe.

— Phase 01

Site Walk & Scope

An on-site visit, a clear conversation about scope, materials and timeline, and a written proposal before anything is signed or paid.

— Phase 02

Initial Deposit

A modest deposit reserves your project window and begins material specification. Schedule and prep work are confirmed in writing.

— Phase 03

Active Work

Materials arrive, the workspace is prepared, and work begins per the schedule. Progress payments are tied to clear milestones — never to a calendar date.

— Phase 04

Final Walk & Closeout

A walk-through, punch list, clean handover and final invoice. Documentation is provided and the relationship continues for future work.
