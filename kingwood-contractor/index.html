Why PGC

What makes us different isn't a tagline — it's how we run every single project.

01

Written Scope Before Any Deposit

You get a written proposal — what's included, what isn't, materials, timeline and cost — before you write a single check. No surprises after demo starts.

02

One Contractor, One Contact

You're not handed off to a crew you've never met. The person you call is the person overseeing the work — from the first site visit to the final walk-through.

03

Milestone Payments, Not Open Bills

Payments follow the work, not a calendar. You pay at defined milestones that you've already agreed to — never a lump sum upfront or a surprise invoice at the end.

04

We Finish What We Start

We don't disappear mid-project, push your job for a bigger one, or leave punch lists open for weeks. If we commit to a project, we see it through.

05

Clean Worksites, Respectful Access

We work in people's homes and businesses. Occupied spaces get protected, work areas get contained, and the site gets cleaned up — every day, not just at the end.

06

Licensed, Insured, Accountable

Fully licensed and insured on every project, residential or commercial. No workarounds, no unlicensed subs, no liability left sitting on the homeowner.
