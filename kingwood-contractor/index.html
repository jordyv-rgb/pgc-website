Roofing & Exterior Repairs

Roof and exterior repairs assessed honestly and scoped before any work begins.

From residential roof repairs and storm damage to commercial flat-roof and TPO repair scopes, PGC handles roofing and exterior envelope work with the same organized process as everything else. We diagnose the actual problem — not just the symptom — and put it in writing.
