The Division

PGC Flooring exists to give one type of project the focus it deserves: floors. As a division of Prominent General Contractors LLC, we draw on the parent firm's processes, scheduling and trade relationships — but operate as a dedicated practice for clients whose project is the flooring itself.

Most of our work is premium waterproof luxury vinyl plank — installed with proper subfloor preparation, manufacturer-spec acclimation and tooling, and the small details (transitions, undercuts, baseboard reset) that separate a long-lived install from a short one.

For clients with broader scopes — kitchens, interior remodels, or full renovations — we work alongside the rest of the Prominent General Contractors practice to keep the project under one accountable team.

01 — Services

Focused flooring services for residential and commercial interiors.

— 01

Residential Waterproof LVP

Whole-home and single-room luxury vinyl plank installations. Ideal for kitchens, baths, mudrooms, hallways and high-traffic living spaces where water and wear matter.

— 02

Subfloor Preparation

Self-leveling, moisture remediation, and substrate correction — the foundation work that determines whether a finished floor lays flat and lasts. Done before plank goes down, not after.

— 03

Commercial Flooring

Light commercial installations for offices, retail, and hospitality interiors. Specified for the loading the space will see and sequenced to limit disruption to ongoing operations.

— 04

Maintenance & Repair

Targeted flooring repair for completed homes — board replacements, threshold work, joint and seam corrections, and finish touch-ups handled cleanly.

02 — Approach

The same phased process used by the parent firm — scoped for flooring projects.

Every Prominent General Contractors project is organized around defined milestones rather than open-ended billing. PGC Flooring follows the same structure, scaled to the size of the work. Payments are accepted via Zelle, ACH, and credit or debit card through Stripe.

— Phase 01

Consultation & Scope

An on-site visit, square-footage measurement, material direction, and a written proposal that defines the scope before anything is signed.

— Phase 02

Initial Deposit

A modest deposit reserves your install window and begins material specification. Schedule, lead times and prep work are confirmed in writing.

— Phase 03

Material Delivery & Installation

Material arrives and acclimates, the workspace is prepared, and installation begins. Progress payment is tied to a clear milestone in the work — never to a calendar.

— Phase 04

Completion & Final Walk

A walk-through, punch list, clean handover and final payment. Warranty terms are documented and the relationship continues for any future work.
