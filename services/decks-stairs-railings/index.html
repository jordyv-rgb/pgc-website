Decks, Stairs & Railings

PGC plans outdoor walking surfaces and access details around the site, intended use, and existing structure.

What the work can include

New decks, repairs and refreshes, stairs, railings, and connections to covered porches or patios as defined in the estimate.

How we plan the project

We inspect the area and any existing structure, discuss dimensions and material preferences, then document the framing, surface, stair, and railing scope.

What shapes the estimate

Ground conditions, structural condition, height, layout, material choice, demolition, access, and finish details affect the estimate.

Common questions

Can an existing deck be repaired?

That depends on the condition of framing, connections, surfaces, and railings. An on-site review is needed.

Can stairs and railings be part of the project?

Yes, they can be included when defined in the written scope.
