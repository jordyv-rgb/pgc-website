Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling

PGC coordinates kitchens and bathrooms as complete projects, from the early scope conversation to the finish details. Work may include cabinetry, vanities, tile, showers, flooring, doors, trim, and painting.

What the work can include

Cabinet and vanity installation, tile, shower and tub areas, flooring, drywall, painting, doors, trim, and related repairs as defined in the project scope.

How we plan the project

We review the space and goals, discuss layout and materials, document site conditions, and prepare a written scope. Material choices and the work sequence are coordinated before installation.

What shapes the estimate

Layout changes, wet-area conditions, material selections, demolition, repair needs, and access affect the scope and estimate.

Project evidence

PGC's residential project archive documents kitchen, shower, vanity, tub, and interior finish work from one remodel.

Common questions

Can we keep parts of the existing room?

Often a scope can be planned around what stays. We need to inspect the room and identify any hidden repair or compatibility issues.

How do material choices affect the schedule?

Cabinetry, tile, fixtures, and other selections affect ordering and sequencing. We discuss those choices during scoping.
