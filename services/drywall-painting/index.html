Drywall Repairs, Finishing & Painting

PGC handles wall and ceiling updates as part of repairs, remodels, and interior build-outs. The intended finish and existing surface condition guide the work.

What the work can include

Drywall repair or installation, patching, finishing, texture coordination, surface preparation, and interior painting where included in the written scope.

How we plan the project

We inspect the affected areas, identify the likely repair and prep steps, discuss the desired appearance, then define the sequence and materials in the estimate.

What shapes the estimate

Damage extent, access, moisture or substrate conditions, texture matching, paint selection, and adjacent finish work can change the scope.

Project evidence

PGC's residential remodel archive shows finished interior spaces; the archive alone does not establish a separate drywall-only project.

Common questions

Will a patch be invisible?

The appearance depends on the existing texture, lighting, and paint. We review matching expectations during scoping.

Can you paint after a repair?

Painting can be included with drywall work when specified in the estimate.
