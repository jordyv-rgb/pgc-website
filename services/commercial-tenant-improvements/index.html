Commercial Tenant Improvements & Interior Build-Outs

PGC works with tenants, owners, and property managers to define interior improvements around the space's intended use and operational needs.

What the work can include

Interior build-outs, metal framing, drywall, painting, flooring, doors, and related finish work as specified in a project-specific scope.

How we plan the project

We discuss the intended use, review the existing space and documents available, identify coordination needs, and prepare a written scope. Work sequencing is planned around access and operations.

What shapes the estimate

Existing conditions, lease requirements, approvals, access, occupancy, materials, and coordination with other trades affect the scope and estimate.

Common questions

What should I have ready for the first discussion?

A location, intended use, available plans or lease requirements, target scope, and timing constraints help us define the next step.

Can PGC coordinate multiple interior trades?

PGC can scope framing, drywall, painting, flooring, and related finishes together for an interior build-out.
