Gable Patio Covers & Covered Porches

PGC builds outdoor living structures tailored to the home and how the space will be used. A site visit is needed to define the framing, roof, and finish scope.

What the work can include

Gable patio covers, covered porches, framing, roof integration, stairs, railings, and related exterior finish work when included in the project scope.

How we plan the project

We review the existing house and outdoor area, discuss coverage and appearance, document dimensions and site conditions, then prepare a project-specific scope and estimate.

What shapes the estimate

Span, support conditions, roof tie-in, drainage, material choice, access, and any required approvals affect planning and pricing.
