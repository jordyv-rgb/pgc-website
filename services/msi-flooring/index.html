MSI Flooring Supply & Installation

PGC helps clients compare MSI flooring options and plan installation around the condition and use of each space. Product selection and availability are confirmed for each project.

What the work can include

Luxury vinyl plank and other appropriate MSI selections; measurements; subfloor review; preparation; removal; trim; transitions; stairs; and furniture moving when included in the written scope.

How we plan the project

We discuss the rooms and desired finish, measure the areas, review existing flooring and subfloor conditions, confirm the selected product and availability, and prepare a project-specific estimate. Installation details and transitions are agreed before work begins.

What shapes the estimate

The quote depends on product selection, availability, measurements, site conditions, prep, removal, trim, transitions, stairs, and moving services.

Common questions

Is waterproof flooring protected from flooding?

No. Waterproof flooring does not protect against flooding, leaks, or subfloor moisture. Manufacturer warranty terms and exclusions apply.

Can I get a price per square foot?

A useful quote requires the product choice, measurements, and site review. We do not advertise a numerical rate before current product and installation costs are confirmed.
