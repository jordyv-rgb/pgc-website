02 — Service Depth

What a kitchen remodel actually involves, trade by trade.

Cabinet Installation · Cabinet Upgrades

Cabinets that organize the kitchen and set the visual tone.

Cabinet installation defines how the kitchen looks, stores and functions. Prominent General Contractors coordinates layout, box installation, door alignment, hardware and the small adjustments that separate a finished installation from a simple cabinet swap.

  • Full cabinet installation and targeted cabinet upgrade coordination for existing kitchens.
  • Hardware selection, door alignment, trim reveal and consistent margin detail.
  • Upper cabinet height, crown moulding decisions and lighting-ready upper layouts.
  • Selections suited for warm, modern Northeast Houston kitchens and open plans.
Countertops · Quartz · Solid Surface

Countertops that hold up to daily use and anchor the material palette.

Countertop work is coordinated alongside cabinet installation, sink placement, backsplash tile and edge profile decisions. We help with material direction and manage sequencing so the countertop installation lands cleanly within the broader kitchen scope.

  • Quartz and modern solid-surface selections for durability and low maintenance.
  • Sink cutout, faucet knock-out and edge profile coordination.
  • Island and peninsula countertops with seating overhang and clearance decisions.
  • Material choices that age well in active, family-oriented kitchens.
Tile Backsplash · Kitchen Tile

Backsplash tile planned around cabinet lines and countertop material.

A backsplash reads as part of the kitchen or it doesn't. We plan tile layout, pattern direction, grout selection and height in relation to what's above and below — so the finish feels purposeful rather than decorative.

  • Layout planned around cabinet uppers, outlet positions and countertop lines.
  • Subway, stacked, herringbone and field tile patterns suited to the material palette.
  • Grout color and joint width chosen to support the overall finish direction.
  • Integration with countertop edge, trim details and adjacent surfaces.
Kitchen Flooring · Waterproof LVP · Tile

Kitchen flooring that holds up to daily use and connects the open plan.

Kitchen floors handle more traffic than almost any surface in the home. Prominent General Contractors — through PGC Flooring — installs waterproof LVP and tile that handles daily wear, moisture and the transition into living and dining areas without interruption.

  • Waterproof LVP and tile flooring selected for kitchens, open plans and high-traffic areas.
  • Direction, plank rhythm and layout planned against cabinet kickplates and island bases.
  • Baseboard reset, undercuts, transitions and room-to-room continuity.
  • Material guidance from PGC Flooring, the specialized flooring division of PGC.
Kitchen Islands · Layout Planning

Islands and layout decisions that make the kitchen more useful, not more crowded.

Island placement, seating integration and layout planning affect how the kitchen feels every day. We think about traffic flow, prep zones, how the island relates to adjacent cabinetry, and whether the proportions support the room rather than overwhelm it.

  • Island sizing and placement coordinated with kitchen workflow and traffic paths.
  • Seating overhang, bar stool clearance and countertop edge profile decisions.
  • Integration with flooring direction, lighting and cabinet door swing clearances.
  • Workflow planning that reflects how the family actually uses the kitchen each day.
Trim · Finish Carpentry · Interior Details

The finish details that make a kitchen remodel feel resolved, not assembled.

Crown moulding, baseboards, casing, cabinet toe-kick, transition strips and paint-ready surfaces are where a kitchen remodel either finishes cleanly or reveals gaps. We coordinate these details as part of the full scope — not as an afterthought once the main trades are done.

  • Baseboards, casing, shoe moulding and paint-ready trim around the full kitchen perimeter.
  • Cabinet filler strips, toe-kick, crown moulding coordination and reveal consistency.
  • Interior remodeling details that support kitchen, dining and adjacent living areas.
  • Clean finish handoff for paint-ready surfaces and final room walk-through.

05 — Process

A kitchen remodel process built around clear decisions and defined milestones.

Kitchen remodels involve more coordinated decisions than most homeowners expect — cabinet lead times, countertop templating, tile sequencing, appliance rough-ins and flooring last. We sequence the work so each trade lands in the right order and the finish quality isn't compromised by scheduling gaps or last-minute decisions.

— Phase 01

Consultation & Scope

An on-site visit to walk the kitchen, photograph conditions, discuss priorities and define a scope that covers what needs to change without overbuilding the space.

— Phase 02

Selections & Proposal

Cabinet direction, countertop material, tile, flooring, hardware and finish details organized into a clear proposal with itemized scope and sequencing notes.

— Phase 03

Sequenced Build

Work sequenced around cabinet delivery, countertop templating, tile prep, flooring installation and trim so no trade is waiting on another or redoing completed work.

— Phase 04

Final Walk & Handoff

A final kitchen walk-through, punch list, cleanup and handoff so the remodel ends cleanly rather than trailing off into small unfinished items weeks after the main work.
