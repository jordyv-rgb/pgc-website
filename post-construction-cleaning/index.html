Why a Dedicated Final Clean

A finished build and a move-in-ready space are not the same thing. Framing dust, drywall residue, sawdust and adhesive overspray settle into places a general sweep never reaches — and they show up fastest on the surfaces a project just spent money finishing.

Prominent Cleaners, the cleaning division of Prominent General Contractors LLC, provides the detailed final clean that closes out a construction or remodeling project properly. For PGC clients, it can be added directly to the project scope. For other contractors and property owners, it's available as a standalone service.

Design and material inspiration — final-detail cleaning reference.

What's Included

A final clean scoped to what construction actually leaves behind.

This isn't a general janitorial visit — it's a detail pass built around what a construction or remodeling project generates: fine dust, finish residue, protective-film adhesive and debris in places standard cleaning skips.

  • Full dust and debris removal, all surfaces
  • Window, sill and track detailing
  • Cabinet and drawer interior wipe-down
  • Light fixture and HVAC vent detailing
  • Floor detail cleaning by surface type
  • Baseboard, trim and door casing wipe-down
  • Bathroom and fixture detailing
  • Protective film and label/adhesive removal
  • Final walkthrough-ready presentation

Who calls for a post-construction clean.

Anyone closing out a build in Greater Houston who needs the space to actually look finished before someone else sees it.
