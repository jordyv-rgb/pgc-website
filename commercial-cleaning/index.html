About Prominent Cleaners

Build it. Improve it. Clean it. Hand it over ready.

Prominent Cleaners is Prominent General Contractors' commercial and post-construction cleaning service — built on the same principle as our construction division: a written scope, one accountable team, and a space that's genuinely ready when we say it is. We work with property managers, commercial landlords, business owners, tenants and general contractors across Greater Houston who need a final clean, a turnover clean, or ongoing commercial cleaning they don't have to double-check.

Because Prominent Cleaners and Prominent General Contractors are the same company, a PGC construction or remodeling project can move straight into final cleaning with no new vendor to vet, no gap in accountability, and no separate contract to chase down.

01 — Post-Construction Final Cleaning

The last step of every build — dust, residue and debris removed before anyone moves in.

Construction dust settles everywhere a job touches — ductwork, light fixtures, window tracks, baseboards, cabinet interiors. Prominent Cleaners handles the detailed final clean that gets a newly built or remodeled space ready for occupancy, not just swept.

For PGC construction and remodeling clients, final cleaning can be added directly to the project scope — one contractor, one handoff, no second vendor to schedule.

  • Full dust and debris removal
  • Window, sill and track detailing
  • Cabinet and drawer interior wipe-down
  • Light fixture and vent detailing
  • Floor detail cleaning by surface type
  • Baseboard and trim wipe-down
  • Bathroom and fixture detailing
  • Final walkthrough-ready presentation

Commercial lobby reference — recurring cleaning programs.

02 — Commercial Office Cleaning

Recurring cleaning for offices that need a consistent standard, not a rotating schedule.

Prominent Cleaners offers recurring commercial cleaning for offices, suites and small business locations across Greater Houston — scheduled around your hours, with a consistent scope every visit.

Programs are set up around what the space actually needs: nightly, weekly or periodic, with a defined checklist so quality doesn't depend on which crew shows up.

  • Nightly or weekly office cleaning
  • Reception and common-area cleaning
  • Restroom sanitation and restocking
  • Break room and kitchen cleaning
  • Trash removal and recycling
  • Floor care by surface type
  • Glass and interior window cleaning
  • Defined recurring checklist per visit

Vacant suite reference — tenant turnover cleaning.

03 — Tenant Turnover & Make-Ready Cleaning

A genuinely lease-ready suite — not just an empty one.

Between tenants, a suite needs more than a quick sweep. Prominent Cleaners provides turnover cleaning that pairs naturally with PGC's make-ready and property turnover work — repairs and cleaning coordinated as one scope, so property managers aren't juggling two vendors on the same timeline.

  • Full move-out deep clean
  • Cabinet, closet and storage detailing
  • Appliance interior cleaning
  • Floor detail by surface type
  • Wall and switch-plate spot cleaning
  • Fixture and vent detailing
  • Coordinated scheduling with make-ready repairs
  • Documented final walkthrough
04 — Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Opening a new location or closing one out — either way, the space should be right.

Retail, salon, restaurant and office tenants preparing to open a new location need the space fully detailed before inventory, fixtures and staff arrive. Prominent Cleaners provides move-in ready cleaning timed to your opening schedule — and the same detail-level clean on the way out, so a move-out inspection or deposit return isn't held up by cleaning.

  • Pre-opening move-in cleaning
  • Move-out deep cleaning
  • Fixture and shelving detail
  • Floor and surface preparation
  • Glass, mirror and display cleaning
  • Scheduled around your opening date
